Disclosures

How Gridleaf makes money, which links are affiliate links, and what we do (and don't do) with your data. In short: free pages stay free, our own templates and some supply links pay for them (ads will too, once Google approves the site), and we don't run analytics.

Affiliate links (Amazon Associates)

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Gridleaf Studio is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

Some pages end with a Helpful supplies box, and a few guides mention supplies in the text. Those links go to Amazon search results; in the box they're marked “Amazon ↗”, and guides that link to Amazon say so at the top. If you buy something after clicking one, Amazon may pay us a small commission. It costs you nothing extra, and Amazon's reports show us which items were ordered, not who ordered them.

We keep these links honest:

  • They are suggestions of what to look for, not reviews. We don't claim to have tested the products, so compare options and read reviews yourself.
  • We link to search results rather than single products, and we never show Amazon prices or ratings, which change all the time.
  • Affiliate links only ever appear on this website: never inside our PDFs, downloads, products or emails.
  • Affiliate links are marked as sponsored for search engines and open in a new tab.
  • The supplies box always comes after the free download or the main content, never in front of it.

Our own products

Gridleaf Studio makes the free printables, calculators and guides on this site, and also sells its own templates: digital and printable planners, budget spreadsheets, templates for Notion, worksheets, resume templates, wall art, puzzle packs, garden planners and website themes. Buttons such as “Get the planner” lead to our own shops at gridleafstudio.gumroad.com and payhip.com/GridleafStudio, which handle checkout. When we recommend one of our templates, we're recommending our own work.

The free downloads are free for personal use, with no sign-up and no email required.

Privacy and ads

  • This site uses no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. There are no accounts, sign-ups, comment forms or newsletters here, so we don't collect names or email addresses.
  • Ads are not shown yet. We have applied to Google AdSense, so most pages (never the games pages) load Google's AdSense script while Google reviews the site; loading it sends your IP address and the address of the page to Google. Before ads are shown, visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for their consent through Google's consent message.
  • Once ads run: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info (in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu). Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Privacy and cookie settings (the link at the bottom of every page): there is nothing to switch on or off yet, because this site sets no cookies of its own and no ads are running. When Google's consent message goes live, that link will reopen it so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • The calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.
  • The free games save your progress in your browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • Fonts are served from this site itself, so loading a page doesn't contact Google Fonts or any other font service.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses and request details to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. The code is stored on GitHub.
  • Third parties, including Amazon, may place and read cookies in your browser when you follow a link to their site, for example so Amazon can credit a purchase to our link. Their own privacy policies apply there. The same is true of our Gumroad and Payhip shops.

Your rights (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

The legal bases for the processing described above, under the GDPR and the UK GDPR:

  • Delivering and protecting the site (Cloudflare's processing of IP addresses and request details): our legitimate interest in running a fast, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Ad cookies and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), asked before any ads are shown. You can withdraw it at any time through Privacy and cookie settings; withdrawing doesn't affect anything that happened before.
  • Saved game progress and anything you type into a calculator stay on your device. We never receive them.

You have the right to access personal data about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one in the country where you live or work (in the UK, the ICO; in Switzerland, the FDPIC).

We keep no visitor records of our own, so the data described here is held by the providers named above, and you can also use their own tools: Google's Ads Settings, Google's data and privacy controls and Cloudflare's privacy policy.
