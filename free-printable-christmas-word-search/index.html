How to get the most out of it

  • Give the easy page to kids aged about 5 to 8, and the hard page to older kids, teens and adults.
  • Find a word's first letter, then check the eight letters around it for the second one.
  • In the hard puzzle, start with long words like FRANKINCENSE and GINGERBREAD; they're easier to spot.
  • Slip the pages into sheet protectors and use dry-erase markers to play again and again.

Free printable Christmas word search FAQ

What ages is it for?

The easy puzzle (12 words on a 12 × 12 grid, across and down only) suits ages 5 to 8. The hard puzzle (24 words on an 18 × 18 grid, in all eight directions) is for older kids, teens and adults.

Are the answers included?

Yes. Page 3 has both answer keys with every word highlighted.

Can I use it at school or at a party?

Yes. Print as many copies as you need for your own home, classroom or party.

Can every word be found?

Yes. Each puzzle was checked by computer: every word appears exactly once, so the answer key is unambiguous.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
