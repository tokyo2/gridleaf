How to get the most out of it

  • Copy last year's list in early November and update anyone who has moved.
  • Write cards in batches of ten with a cup of tea. It goes faster than you think.
  • Tick Received as cards arrive, and add anyone you forgot.
  • Check your postal service's last mailing dates for the holidays, especially for cards going abroad.

Free printable Christmas card list FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

How many names fit on the page?

23 rows on US Letter and 25 on A4. Print a second copy for a longer list.

Is it dated?

No. Write the year at the top and keep each year's list in your planner.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
