    Ways to use it in class

    • Monday practice or homework. Paste the week's list and print a class set in a minute. Name and date lines are already on.
    • Differentiate in seconds. Make the same list on Easy (across and down) for beginning readers and on Hard (every direction, also backwards) for strong spellers. Large print helps students who need bigger text.
    • Early finishers and centers. Keep a few puzzles from past lists for review, with the answer key at the center for self-checking.
    • Any subject. Vocabulary for science, social studies or a novel study works too. Phrases are fine: the grid drops the spaces.

    The maker skips words shorter than 3 letters and tells you about any word that's part of another word on the list (like cat and catch), because it would appear twice in the grid.

    FAQ

    Can I print class sets?

    Yes. Print as many copies as you need for your own classes and students. Please don't sell the puzzles.

    Is it safe to type student names?

    The words you type are not sent anywhere: the puzzle and the PDF are made in your browser, and nothing is saved when you close the page.

    Which level should I use for my grade?

    As a rough guide: Easy for kindergarten to grade 1, Medium for grades 2 to 3, and Hard from grade 4. You know your class best, so try a level and adjust.

    Why was a word left out?

    Words need 3 or more letters and must fit the grid. If one word is part of another (cat, catch), the shorter one is left out because it would appear twice. The maker lists every word it left out and why.
