How to get the most out of it

  • Go through every recipe on your menu and add each ingredient to its section.
  • Check the pantry for spices and baking basics before you shop. They're the easiest things to buy twice.
  • Buy the turkey early: a frozen bird needs several days to thaw in the fridge.
  • Shop for non-perishables the weekend before and leave only fresh items for the last trip.
