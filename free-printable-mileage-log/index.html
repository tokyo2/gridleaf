How to get the most out of it

  • Write down the odometer reading before you set off and again when you arrive.
  • Mark each trip B (business) or P (personal) so the totals are easy to split.
  • Keep the log in your glove box with a pen.
  • Check the current IRS standard mileage rate (or your country's rate). This log is a record, not tax advice.
