Gifts for Grandparents & Seniors (2026)

Many grandparents say they don't want anything, and mean it. The gifts that land are usually about time, family and small comforts: a daily puzzle that's easy to see, photos of the people they love and things that make home a little easier.

Gift guide FAQ

What do you get grandparents who say they don't want anything?

Time and memories: photos of the family, a calendar with everyone's birthday, a visit or a call they can look forward to. Small comforts, like a warm throw or a good reading light, also tend to get used every day.

Are large-print puzzles a good gift for seniors?

For anyone who enjoys a daily puzzle, yes: large print makes them easier to see and less tiring. Our large-print puzzle tips show how to print puzzles bigger and cut glare.

How do I set up a digital photo frame as a gift?

Set it up before you give it: connect it to Wi-Fi if it needs it, install its app on family phones and add the first photos, so it works as soon as it's plugged in.

How did you choose these ideas?

We picked kinds of gifts that suit grandparents and older relatives and wrote down what to look for in each one. We haven't tested specific brands or products. The links open Amazon search results, so you can compare options, prices and reviews yourself.
