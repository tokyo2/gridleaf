Tips

  • Tall letters touch the top line, small letters reach the dashed middle line, and tails hang below.
  • Trace slowly with a thick pencil or crayon, and praise neat effort over speed.
  • Slide the page into a sheet protector to practice again and again with a dry-erase marker.
