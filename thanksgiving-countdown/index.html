Countdown FAQ

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

US Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November: Thursday, November 26, 2026. In 2027 it falls on Thursday, November 25, and in 2028 on Thursday, November 23.

How is the Thanksgiving countdown calculated?

It counts down to midnight at the start of Thanksgiving in your own time zone, using your device's clock.

What are 'sleeps'?

The number of nights left, including tonight, the way kids count it.
