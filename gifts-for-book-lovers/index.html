Gifts for Book Lovers & Readers (2026)

Buying a book for a reader is a gamble: they may have read it, or already own it. These ideas make reading more comfortable or help them remember what they've read, so they rarely clash with what's already on the shelf.

Reading comfort

  • Rechargeable book light · Amazon ↗

    Clips onto a book for reading in bed without keeping anyone awake. Look for warm light and several brightness levels.

  • Book stand · Amazon ↗

    Holds a book open hands-free at the table, for cookbooks, study books and heavy hardbacks.

  • Reading pillow with arms · Amazon ↗

    Props them up in bed or on the floor with proper back support. A side pocket for glasses or a phone helps.

  • Thumb page holder · Amazon ↗

    A small ring that holds a paperback open with one hand, handy on the commute or with a drink in the other.

Remembering every book

  • Reading journal · Amazon ↗

    Space for ratings, favorite quotes and what to read next. Start with our free reading log, or compare bound journals.

  • Magnetic bookmarks · Amazon ↗

    They clip over the page, so they don't fall out, and a set covers several books on the go at once.

  • Transparent annotation tabs · Amazon ↗

    Mark favorite lines and passages without writing in the book itself.

  • Personalized book embosser · Amazon ↗

    Presses their name into the title page, like a library stamp, so borrowed books find their way home. Order early: personalized items take longer to make and ship.

For the bookshelf and the bag

  • Heavy bookends · Amazon ↗

    Weighted or non-slip bookends keep a half-full shelf from toppling over.

  • Padded book sleeve · Amazon ↗

    Protects a paperback or hardback in a bag, so covers don't get bent.

  • E-reader cover · Amazon ↗

    Search with their exact e-reader model, because covers are made for one model's size and buttons.

  • Book lover mug · Amazon ↗

    For the tea or coffee that goes with a good chapter. Look for a large, dishwasher-safe mug.

Something special

  • Clothbound classic · Amazon ↗

    A beautiful edition of a favorite they already love, chosen for the shelf as much as for reading.

  • Kraft paper and twine for a blind date with a book · Amazon ↗

    Wrap a book you think they'll love and write three clues on the front instead of the title.

Budget tips

Keep gift shopping on budget

  • Set a budget for each person and write it on the free printable Christmas gift list before you shop.
  • Prices change all the time, so we don't list them. On the search results page, use the price filter or sort by price to stay inside your budget.
  • Many stores run sales around Black Friday (November 27, 2026) and Cyber Monday (November 30). Make your list first, so you buy what you planned rather than what's on offer.
  • Not sure what they've read? Ask for their wishlist, or choose a comfort gift instead of a book.

Gift guide FAQ

What do you get a book lover who has everything?

Something that makes reading more comfortable, like a book light, a book stand or a reading pillow, or something that helps them remember what they read, like a reading journal or tracker. Neither depends on knowing what's on their shelf.

Should I give a book as a gift?

If you know their taste or their wishlist, yes. Otherwise a special edition of a book they already love is a safer choice than a new title they may have read.

What is a reading journal?

A notebook for the books you read: ratings, favorite quotes, thoughts and a list of what to read next. Our printable reading journal has 33 pages: 24 book reviews, a 60-book reading list, TBR list, reading goals and stats. US Letter and A4 included.

How did you choose these ideas?
