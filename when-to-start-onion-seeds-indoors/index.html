Why 10 to 12 weeks before the frost?

Onion seedlings are slow to reach a size worth planting, so they are usually the first seeds of the year. K-State Research and Extension says onions can take 10 to 12 weeks to reach transplant size, and NDSU Extension's chart gives 10 weeks before the last frost. Onions are hardy, so unlike tomatoes and peppers they go out before the last frost: NDSU gives 2 weeks before it, and Iowa State Extension says to sow 8 to 10 weeks before you plan to plant them out.

How to start onion seeds indoors

  1. Sow thickly in a tray or in cells, a few seeds per cell, about ¼ inch (6 mm) deep in moist seed-starting mix.
  2. Keep them warm until they sprout, then move them somewhere cooler and bright.
  3. Give them strong light, with the lamp about 4 to 6 inches above the seedlings, as Iowa State Extension suggests. Warmth and weak light make them thin and floppy.
  4. Many growers trim the tops back to about 3 to 4 inches when they flop over. It keeps the seedlings sturdy.
  5. Harden them off for 7 to 10 days, bringing them in if a hard freeze is forecast.
  6. Plant them out about 4 to 6 inches apart in rows, at the depth they grew in the tray.

NDSU Extension's chart gives 75–85°F (24–29°C) for germination, with seedlings up in about 5 to 8 days. After that, Iowa State Extension recommends growing them cooler, at 55–65°F (13–18°C).

Using your hardiness zone?

USDA hardiness zones are based on average winter low temperatures, not on frost dates, so two gardens in the same zone can have last frosts weeks apart. Look up your own average last spring frost date (your local extension service lists it, and many frost-date tools find it by ZIP or postal code), then count back from it.

Onion seed-starting FAQ

How many weeks before the last frost should I start onion seeds?

Start onion seeds indoors 10 to 12 weeks before your average last spring frost date. Plant out 2 to 4 weeks before your last frost date: onions are hardy and take a light frost.

When should I start onion seeds indoors in zone 5, 6 or 7?

Use your last frost date rather than your zone. USDA hardiness zones are based on average winter low temperatures, not on frost dates, so two gardens in the same zone can have last frosts weeks apart. For example, with a last frost on Apr 20, sow between Jan 26 and Feb 9.

When can I plant onion seedlings outside?

Plant out 2 to 4 weeks before your last frost date: onions are hardy and take a light frost. Harden them off for 7 to 10 days first.

How long do onion seeds take to germinate?

About 5 to 8 days at 75–85°F (24–29°C), the range in NDSU Extension's chart. After they sprout, grow them cooler, at about 55–65°F (13–18°C).

Which onion variety should I grow from seed?

Pick a day-length type that suits where you live: long-day onions are for northern gardens, short-day onions for southern ones and intermediate (day-neutral) types for the middle. Seed catalogs label each variety.

Seeds or onion sets?

Sets are quicker and easier. Seeds take longer but cost less per plant and give you far more varieties to choose from.
