How to get the most out of it

  • Start small: 3 to 6 chores is plenty for younger kids.
  • Write the chores together so your child knows exactly what each one means.
  • Agree on the reward before the week starts, and keep it small and regular.
  • Print a fresh chart every Sunday and stick it on the fridge.

Free printable chore chart FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

What age is it for?

Any age from about 3 up. Younger kids can use stickers instead of ticks.

Can I use it for more than one child?

Print one chart per child, or try the family chore chart in the full set.
