How to get the most out of it

  • Toddlers and preschoolers: you write the blanks, they tick the boxes and draw their wish.
  • Ages 6 and up: pick two or three of the writing ideas on page 2 to get started.
  • Ask them to add something kind they did this year, or a wish for someone else.
  • Take a photo of the letter before it goes to the North Pole. It makes a lovely keepsake.
  • Fill in Santa's reply (page 3) in your best Santa handwriting, fold it and leave it next to the empty cookie plate.

What to write in a letter to Santa

Page 2 prints these ideas at the top of the page. Two or three are plenty:

  • Something great that happened this year
  • Something kind you did for someone
  • Something you're proud of learning
  • What you're wishing for (and why)
  • A question for Santa or the reindeer
  • Something you'd like to wish for someone else

Make it a little tradition

  • Write the letter on the same day each year, like the first weekend of December.
  • Keep each year's letter in an envelope with the date. Reading old letters together is half the fun.
  • Add a wish for someone else, like a grandparent, a friend or the family pet.
  • Leave the reply from Santa (page 3) next to the empty cookie plate on Christmas morning.

Free printable letter to Santa template FAQ

What should kids write in a letter to Santa?

Start with Dear Santa, then a few lines about their year, something they're proud of or a kind thing they did, two or three wishes, a question for Santa or the reindeer, and sign it with love. Page 2 lists these ideas so kids can choose.

What age is it for?

Page 1 suits ages about 3 to 6 with a grown-up helping. Page 2 is for kids who can write a few sentences, usually about 6 and up.

How do I mail a letter to Santa?

Several national postal services run Santa letter programs at Christmas, and some even send replies. Each one sets its own address, deadline and rules every year, so check your postal service's website before you post it. Or keep it at home and leave it by the tree on Christmas Eve.

Is there a letter from Santa too?

Yes. Page 3 is a reply from Santa with blanks for a grown-up to fill in, plus an Official Nice List badge with the child's name.

Can I use it at school?

Yes. Print as many copies as you need for your own home or classroom.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
