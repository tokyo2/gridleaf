Folio: an Astro ecommerce theme for selling digital products

Folio is a calm, fast storefront theme for Astro 7, made for people who sell templates, planners, presets, courses, ebooks and other downloads. You get a product grid, product and bundle pages, a pricing page, a blog, docs and site search, and every buy button goes to the checkout you already use.

What's in the theme

Sell from day one

  • A product grid, category pages and product pages with a buy button for any checkout: Gumroad, Payhip, Lemon Squeezy, Stripe Payment Links, Ko-fi or your own cart
  • Sale prices, free and pay-what-you-want products, and quick facts beside the buy button
  • A bundle layout that lists what's included and works out the value and the saving
  • A pricing page with tiers, plus testimonials and an FAQ
  • Optional Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy pop-up checkout

Grow an audience

  • A blog in Markdown or MDX, with tags, reading time, pagination and RSS
  • A newsletter signup that works with any provider's embed form, and a contact form for any form service or Netlify Forms
  • A docs section with sidebar and table of contents, for guides, help pages or course notes
  • Site search that needs no external service
  • Docs, pricing and search each have an on/off switch

Built properly

  • Built for Astro 7. One config file holds your name, colours, menu, socials, texts and sections, and products are simple Markdown files
  • Light and dark mode with 3 colour presets (sage, clay, ocean), each meeting WCAG 2.2 AA contrast
  • SEO built in: meta tags, Open Graph, sitemap, robots.txt and structured data for products (Product with Offer), posts and breadcrumbs
  • Almost no JavaScript, optimised images and self-hosted fonts
  • Static output you can host free on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify, with step-by-step guides

In Lighthouse 13.6.0 tests of the demo (10 pages, November 2026, on a local server), it scored 99–100 for performance on mobile and 100 on desktop, and 100 for accessibility, best practices and SEO.

A storefront, not a shopping cart

Most people selling digital products already have a checkout: Gumroad, Payhip, Lemon Squeezy or a Stripe Payment Link. Folio doesn't replace it. It gives your products a home that looks considered, loads quickly and is built for search (meta tags, sitemap and product structured data), and hands buyers to that checkout to pay and download. There is nothing to run on a server and no payment data on your site.

If you sell physical stock with shipping and inventory, you need a full ecommerce platform instead; Folio is built for downloads, courses and other digital products.

Light, dark and three presets

Sage, clay and ocean colour presets, each with a light and a dark mode that meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Swap the fonts, colours and sections in one config file, write products and posts in Markdown, and deploy a static site.

Folio FAQ

Is Folio a full ecommerce theme with a cart?

No. Folio is a storefront: product, bundle and pricing pages whose buy buttons link to the checkout you already use (Gumroad, Payhip, Lemon Squeezy, Stripe Payment Links, Ko-fi or your own cart). That checkout takes the payment and delivers the files, so the site itself stays static, fast and free to host. It has no shopping cart or customer accounts.

Which version of Astro does it use?

Folio is built for Astro 7 and needs Node.js 22.23.4 or newer.

Where can I host it?

Anywhere that serves a static site. Cloudflare Pages and Netlify host it free, and the docs walk through both. GitHub Pages works from a private repository on a paid GitHub plan.

What's the difference between Personal and Pro?

Personal ($49) covers one website that you own and run. Pro ($99) covers any number of websites, including sites you build for paying clients. Neither licence lets you resell or publish Folio itself as a theme or template.

Do I need to know how to code?

A little. You should be comfortable running npm install and npm run dev in a terminal and editing Markdown and one config file. The README walks through setup and deployment.

Is Folio made by the Astro team?

No. Folio is an independent theme for Astro made by Gridleaf Studio, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Astro project.

Was AI used to make it?

Yes. Folio was made by Gridleaf Studio with AI assistance: the code, documentation and demo text were written with Anthropic's Claude. Every release goes through automated build, link, contrast and phone-layout checks before it ships.
