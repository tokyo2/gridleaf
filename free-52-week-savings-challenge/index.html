How to get the most out of it

  • Start any week of the year. Write the start date at the top.
  • Too much in December? Do the weeks in reverse and save $52 first while motivation is high.
  • Move the money to a separate savings account every week so it's out of sight.
  • Fell behind? Double up next week, or tick off any unchecked amount you can afford.

Free printable 52-week savings challenge FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

How much do you save in the 52-week challenge?

$1,378. That's 1 + 2 + … + 52 dollars.

Can I start in the middle of the year?

Yes. Week 1 is whenever you start; the challenge runs for 52 weeks from there.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
