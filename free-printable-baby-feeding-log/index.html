How to get the most out of it

  • Keep the page and a pen wherever you usually feed the baby.
  • Circle L, R or B for left side, right side or bottle.
  • Add up the totals before bed; it makes patterns easy to spot.
  • Take a few pages to checkups. It's a record, not medical advice.

Free printable baby feeding log FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

What does the page track?

16 rows for time, feed type, amount (oz or ml), minutes, wet and dirty diapers, sleep and notes, plus daily totals.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's for your own records. Follow your pediatrician's guidance on feeding and sleep.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
