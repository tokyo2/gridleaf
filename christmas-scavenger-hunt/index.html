How to get the most out of it

  • For ages 4 to 5, use 5 or 6 clues and read them aloud. From about 6, kids can read the rhymes themselves.
  • Hide the clues after the kids are in bed, and check the list on page 4 as you go.
  • Several kids? Let them take turns reading each clue aloud, so the fastest reader doesn't win every time.
  • For a party, print one copy of page 5 per team and set a 30-minute timer.
  • Slip the clue cards into sheet protectors to use them again next year.

How to set up a Christmas scavenger hunt

  1. Print and cut the 12 clue cards (pages 2 and 3).
  2. Hide the clues using the list on page 4: clue 2 in the fridge, clue 3 in the cookie jar, and so on.
  3. Put the prize under the tree: a small gift, a treat, new pajamas or the last present to open.
  4. Hand over clue 1 and let the kids race. Little ones need a grown-up to read the rhymes aloud.
  5. Make it yours. Skip a clue that doesn't fit your home, or write your own on the 4 blank cards.

Only hide clues in safe places: never in the oven, a running dryer or anywhere high.

Free printable Christmas scavenger hunt FAQ

How does a Christmas scavenger hunt with clues work?

Each clue card describes the hiding place of the next card. The kids get clue 1, find clue 2 in the place it describes, and keep going until the final clue leads them to the prize under the Christmas tree.

What ages is it for?

The picture hunt suits ages about 3 to 6. The rhyming clues work for about 5 to 10, with a grown-up reading for younger kids. The party hunt on page 5 is for teens and adults.

Are the answers included?

Yes. Page 4 lists where to hide every clue, and this page shows each answer under its clue.

Can I use it indoors?

Yes. All 12 clues lead to places inside the house, like the fridge, the bookshelf and the doormat. The picture hunt also works outside on a walk to see the lights.

Can I write my own clues?

Yes. Page 4 has 4 blank clue cards, so you can replace any clue that doesn't fit your home.

Is there a scavenger hunt for adults?

Yes. Page 5 has 24 tasks for teens and adults, worth 1 to 3 points each, such as finding an ornament older than you or recreating a scene from a Christmas movie.

Which paper size should I print?
