Sinking Funds: What They Are and How to Start

A sinking fund is money you set aside each month for an expense you know is coming: car repairs, gifts, annual fees. When the bill arrives, the money is already there.

How much to save

Estimate the yearly cost and divide by 12. Some typical starting points:

Track them

Seeing the balance grow is what keeps the habit going. The printable budget binder has a sinking funds tracker for six funds, and the budget spreadsheet tracks savings goals automatically.

Sinking funds FAQ

Is a sinking fund the same as an emergency fund?

No. An emergency fund covers the unexpected, like job loss. A sinking fund covers expenses you know are coming but don't pay monthly.

Where should I keep sinking funds?

A separate high-yield savings account works well. Some banks let you create named sub-accounts for each fund.

How many sinking funds should I have?

Start with three to five for your biggest irregular costs. You can add more once the habit sticks.
