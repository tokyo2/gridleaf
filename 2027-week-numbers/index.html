2027 week numbers FAQ

How many weeks are in 2027?

2027 has 52 ISO weeks. Week 1 starts on Monday, Monday, January 4, 2027.

What is the ISO week number?

ISO 8601 weeks start on Monday. Week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of the year, so it can start in late December of the previous year.

Why does week 1 of 2027 start on January 4?

January 1, 2027 is a Friday, so the first Thursday is January 7. The Monday of that week is January 4, which is where ISO week 1 begins. January 1–3, 2027 belong to week 53 of 2026.
