How to play sudoku

Fill the 9 × 9 grid so that every row, every column and every 3 × 3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once. Some numbers are given at the start; you work out the rest. There's no math involved: the numbers are just nine different symbols.

  1. Scan for a number. Pick a number, like 5, and look at each box that doesn't have one yet. Rule out the rows and columns that already contain a 5.
  2. Find the only spot. If just one empty square in a box, row or column can take the 5, write it in.
  3. Fill nearly full lines. A row with 7 or 8 numbers is quick to finish: check which numbers are missing and where they fit.
  4. Use pencil marks. On medium and hard puzzles, write small candidates in the corners of each square, then erase them as you go.
  5. Stuck? Switch numbers. Move on to another number or another box. One new number often unlocks several more.
