Canada holidays FAQ

How many statutory holidays are in Canada in 2027?

There are 10 federal statutory holidays in 2027. Provinces add their own, such as Family Day.

When is Thanksgiving 2027 in Canada?

Canadian Thanksgiving is Monday, October 11, 2027.

When is Victoria Day 2027?

Victoria Day is Monday, May 24, 2027.

When are Christmas and Boxing Day observed in 2027?

Christmas Day is on a Saturday and Boxing Day on a Sunday, so they are generally observed on Monday, December 27 and Tuesday, December 28.
