Gift guide FAQ

What are good stocking stuffers for adults?

Small things they'll use every week: a cable pouch or a power bank for the bag, sticky notes and cable clips for the desk, pantry labels or a fridge list pad for the kitchen. Useful beats novelty when you're filling a stocking for a grown-up.

How do I keep stocking stuffers under $25?

Set the limit before you shop and write it on your gift list. On the search results page, use the price filter or sort by price. Prices change daily, which is why we don't list them here.

Yes. Print a free 2027 calendar, roll it up and tie it with ribbon, or print a whole planner and clip it together. The printables from our studio below cost $5 to $7.
