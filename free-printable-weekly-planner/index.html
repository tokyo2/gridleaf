How to get the most out of it

  • Plan on Sunday evening: write your weekly focus first, then add appointments to each day.
  • Keep each day to 3–5 tasks. Anything else goes in Next week.
  • Use the Priorities box for things that move your goals forward, not just chores.
  • Print a stack, hole-punch them and keep a running weekly binder.

Free printable weekly planner FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

Is it dated?

No. Write the date in the Week of box, so you can use it for any week of any year.

Does the week start on Sunday or Monday?

The US Letter version starts the week on Sunday and the A4 version starts on Monday.

Can I use it in GoodNotes or Notability?

Yes. Import the PDF into GoodNotes, Notability or any PDF app, then duplicate the page whenever you need a fresh one.
