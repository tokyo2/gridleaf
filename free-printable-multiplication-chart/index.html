Tips

  • Start with the easy tables (1, 2, 5, 10), then look for patterns such as 9s, square numbers and the diagonal.
  • Use the blank chart as a weekly challenge: fill in as many squares as you can in 5 minutes.
  • Turn facts around: 3 × 7 is the same as 7 × 3, which halves what you need to learn.
