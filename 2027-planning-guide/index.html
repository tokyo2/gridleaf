How to Plan Your 2027: From Yearly Goals to a Weekly Review

A simple way to plan the year that you'll still be using in March: choose up to three goals, split them into quarterly milestones, plan each month, and keep a 20-minute weekly review. It takes an afternoon to set up, and every step has a free printable.

Plan 2027 in six steps

  1. Look back at 2026. Spend 20 to 30 minutes on what went well, what didn't, and what you want to stop, keep and start. Your best goals for the new year usually come out of this list.
  2. Choose up to three goals for 2027. Brainstorm by life area, pick a word of the year, then circle the three goals that would make the biggest difference. The free 2027 goals worksheet walks you through it on one page.
  3. Split each goal into quarterly milestones. Give each goal one milestone per quarter: something you can finish in about 13 weeks. Q1 runs from Friday, January 1 to Wednesday, March 31, 2027.
  4. Plan each month. On the first of the month, write the fixed dates on a monthly calendar, then choose one to three priorities that move this quarter's milestones forward. Add one or two habits to track if you like.
  5. Review every week. Once a week, take 20 minutes: look back at last week, look at the week ahead, and choose your top three for the next seven days.
  6. Keep each day small. Each morning, pick the one to three tasks that matter most and do those first. Everything else is a bonus.

A 20-minute weekly review

Pick a regular time, such as Sunday evening or Friday afternoon, and answer five questions:

  • What went well last week, and why?
  • What didn't get done? Move it, shrink it or drop it.
  • What's fixed in the week ahead: appointments, deadlines, birthdays?
  • Which three things would make this week a success?
  • Which habit or goal needs a little more attention?

2027 planning FAQ

When should I plan for 2027?

Any time from mid-December to mid-January works. 2027 begins on Friday, January 1, and the first full Monday-to-Sunday week starts on Monday, January 4 (ISO week 1), a natural day to start your weekly reviews.

How many goals should I set for the year?

Three is a good number: enough to cover what matters most, and few enough that you can make real progress on each. Keep other ideas on a someday list.

What's the difference between a goal and a resolution?

A resolution is usually a habit you want to start or stop, like walking every day. A goal has a finish line, like running a 5K by June. Both fit this system: track habits each month and break goals into quarterly milestones.

How many weeks are in 2027?

2027 has 365 days: 52 full weeks plus one day. In ISO week numbering it has 52 weeks, and week 1 starts on Monday, January 4.

Do I need a planner to do this?

No. The free printables on this page are enough to start. A dated planner just keeps every month, week and goal in one place.
