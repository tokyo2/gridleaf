Tips for reaching a savings goal

  • Automate a transfer on payday so the money moves before you can spend it.
  • Keep goal money in a separate high-yield savings account.
  • Round up: saving a little more than the calculated amount gives you a buffer for missed months.
  • Track progress visually — a filled bar is surprisingly motivating.
