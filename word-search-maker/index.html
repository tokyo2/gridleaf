    How to make a word search

    1. Add your words. Type or paste them, one per line or separated by commas, or start from one of 100 ready-made themes and edit it.

    2. Choose the size and difficulty. Small grids and Easy (across and down) suit young kids and quick breaks; big grids and Hard (all 8 directions, also backwards) suit puzzle fans.

    3. Check the preview. Every word is in the grid exactly once. Tick “Show the answers” to see them, or click New layout for a different arrangement of the same words.

    4. Download and print. Pick US Letter or A4 and download a 2-page PDF: the puzzle and the answer key.

    Word search maker FAQ

    Is the word search maker really free?

    Yes. There's no sign-up, no watermark and no limit: make as many puzzles as you like and print them at home.

    Are my words private?

    Yes. The puzzle and the PDF are made in your browser, so the words you type are not sent anywhere: not to us and not to anyone else. Nothing is saved either; when you close the page, your words are gone.

    What do the difficulty levels mean?

    Easy: words run across (left to right) and down. Medium: also diagonally, still reading forwards. Hard: in all 8 directions, including backwards.

    How many words can I use?

    Words need 3 letters or more and can be as long as the grid is wide. A 10 × 10 grid holds up to 10 words, 12 × 12 up to 15, 15 × 15 up to 22, 18 × 18 up to 30 and 20 × 20 up to 36. Large print holds up to 10, 14 and 18 words on 10 × 10, 12 × 12 and 15 × 15 grids.

    Why was one of my words left out?

    The maker tells you why under the preview: the word was too short or too long for the grid, it had numbers (spell them out), it was on the list twice, it's part of a longer word on the list (like ring and rings, which would make it appear twice), or the grid was too full. On Hard, where words also run backwards, a word that's another word backwards (saw and was) or part of one backwards (rat and star) is left out too; choose Easy or Medium to keep both.

    Does every word appear only once?

    Yes. Before you see a puzzle, the maker reads the whole grid in all 8 directions and checks that each word appears exactly once in the directions its level uses. Random filler letters never make an extra copy of a word in any direction, and any filler letter that would spell a common rude word is swapped. Easy and Medium never run backwards, so a pair like was and saw can both be on the list: SAW may also read backwards inside WAS, but no one solving an Easy or Medium puzzle reads backwards.

    Can I print copies for my class, group or party?

    Yes, for personal use: your family, your own classroom, a club, a church group or a party. Print as many copies as you need. Please don't sell the puzzles or the PDFs.

    Which paper sizes can I print?

    US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) and A4. Choose one before you download, and print at 100% (Actual size).

    Is there an answer key?

    Yes. Every PDF has two pages: the puzzle and an answer key that highlights every word.
