Free printable Christmas gift list FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

How many people fit on the list?

About 25 rows on US Letter, each with a gift idea, budget, spent, bought and wrapped column. Print a second copy for big families.

Is it dated?

No. Write the year at the top and use it every Christmas.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
