Tips

  • Tailor the Profile to each job in 2–3 lines: who you are, your years of experience and one result with a number.
  • Start every bullet with an action verb and end with a result: “Reduced wait times by 25% by redesigning the booking process.”
  • Keep your contact details in the body of the page (not in a header or footer), and send a PDF unless the posting asks for Word.

FAQ

Is this template really ATS-friendly?

Yes. It uses one column, real text headings (Profile, Experience, Education, Skills), standard fonts, and no tables, images or text boxes.

How do I open it in Google Docs?

Upload the .docx to Google Drive, right-click it and choose Open with, then Google Docs. You can also use File, then Open, then Upload in Google Docs.
