How to get the most out of it

  • Print on white card stock so the tags are sturdy enough to tie on.
  • Print at 100% (Actual size), not "fit to page", so every tag keeps its size.
  • Type the names into the To and From lines before printing: it's quick, and Santa's tags won't look like your handwriting.
  • Give the black-and-white page to the kids to color while you wrap.
  • No ribbon? Tape or glue the tag flat onto the gift.

How to make the tags

  1. Type or write the names. The To and From lines on pages 1 and 2 are fillable: open the PDF, click a line and type, then print. Or print first and write by hand.
  2. Print on card stock at 100% (Actual size). Plain paper works too if you glue or tape the tag flat onto the gift.
  3. Cut around each circle. The solid outline is the cut line; the dotted ring stays on as a border.
  4. Punch the small circle with a single hole punch and tie the tag on with ribbon, twine or curling ribbon.

Tips for Santa's gifts

  • Type the names on Santa's tags so they don't look like your handwriting.
  • Wrap Santa's gifts in a paper you don't use for your own gifts.
  • Keep page 2 hidden until the kids are asleep on Christmas Eve.

Free printable Christmas gift tags FAQ

Are the gift tags really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for your own gifts or your classroom. No sign-up.

Can I type names on the tags?

Yes. The To and From lines on the color tags and the To lines on the From Santa tags are fillable. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader or a browser such as Chrome or Edge, click a line, type, then print. The words and designs themselves can't be changed.

How big are the tags?

About 2.2 inches (5.5 cm) across, 12 to a page, on both US Letter and A4.

What paper should I use?

White card stock makes the sturdiest tags. Regular printer paper is fine if you glue or tape the tags flat onto the gifts.

Are there black and white gift tags?

Yes. Page 3 has the same 12 designs as line art: print it to save color ink, or let the kids color them.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
