How to get the most out of it

  • Fill in fixed appointments first, then block time for your top 3.
  • Pick only three priorities. If you finish them, the day was a success.
  • Circle the weekday at the top and add the date so pages are easy to file.
  • Tick a water circle for every glass you drink.

Free printable daily planner FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

What hours does the schedule cover?

6 AM to 9 PM, with a light half-hour line between each hour.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.

Can I use it in GoodNotes or Notability?

Yes. Import the PDF into GoodNotes, Notability or any PDF app, then duplicate the page whenever you need a fresh one.
