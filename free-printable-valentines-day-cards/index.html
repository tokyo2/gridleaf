How to get the most out of it

  • Print on white card stock so the cards are sturdy enough to swap and keep.
  • Each sheet makes 8 cards: count the names on the class list and divide by 8 to see how many sheets to print.
  • Let your child write the To and From lines. It's good handwriting practice too.
  • Check the class rules before adding candy or treats, because many classrooms have allergy policies.

Free printable Valentine's Day cards and class list FAQ

When is Valentine's Day 2027?

Valentine's Day 2027 is Sunday, February 14. Because it falls on a weekend, school card exchanges will be on a school day before it, so check the date with your child's teacher. Monday, February 15, 2027 is Presidents' Day.

How many cards are on each page?

Eight, each about 3.6 × 2.2 inches on US Letter (about 9 × 6 cm on A4). Print as many sheets as you need for the whole class.

Should my child bring a card for everyone?

Many classrooms ask for a card for every classmate so nobody feels left out. The class list on page 2 has room for 34 names (40 on A4), with Signed and Packed checkboxes.

Can I print it in black and white?

Yes. The cards are designed in soft colors, but the pictures and lines still print clearly in black and white.

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
