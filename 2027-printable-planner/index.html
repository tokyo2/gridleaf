Which paper sizes are included?

Both. You get a US Letter PDF (8.5 × 11 in) that starts weeks on Sunday and an A4 PDF that starts weeks on Monday.

Does week 1 start on January 1?

Week 1 is the week that contains January 1, 2027, so it starts in late December 2026. No day of the year is missing, and there are 53 weekly pages.

Yes. Each month is its calendar, its goals & habits page and that month's weekly pages, so you can print as you go.
