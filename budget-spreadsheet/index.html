What's inside: 8 sheets that work together

  • Start: a one-page guide to setting it up in about ten minutes.
  • Setup: your income and expense categories and a monthly budget for each one.
  • Transactions: log every income and expense with a category dropdown; the month and year fill in by themselves.
  • Dashboard: pick a month to see income, spending, net savings, savings rate and budget vs actual for every category, with progress bars and a chart.
  • Annual: your whole year by category and month, plus an income vs spending chart.
  • Bills: recurring bills with due dates, marked Paid, Due or Late each month.
  • Savings goals: target, saved so far and target date, so you see how much to save each month.
  • Debt payoff: balance, APR and payment for each debt, with months to payoff, payoff date, total interest and your snowball or avalanche order.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a monthly budget in a spreadsheet without building one from scratch: you type in transactions and the dashboard does the math. It suits a first budget, a family budget, or tracking debt payoff and savings goals in one place.

It's set up for 2027, and you can reuse it: change the year on the Setup sheet and clear the Transactions sheet to start a new year.

Yes. It works in Excel for Windows, Mac and the web, and in Google Sheets.
