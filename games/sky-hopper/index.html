About the game

Sky Hopper is an endless one-tap climber. Your little bird rides the rim of a spinning sky island. Tap to leap straight out from where you stand, so wait until you're facing the next island, then jump and land on it.

  • A guided first run shows your leap path until you get the timing
  • Islands spin faster and drift further apart as you climb
  • Cracked islands crumble after one turn
  • Golden seeds for bonus points
  • Every island is generated to be reachable, checked by simulating the real jump
  • Smooth animation, particles and a sky that darkens to night as you rise
  • Touch, mouse or keyboard; phones and desktops

How to play

  • Tap, click, Space or Up arrow to leap.
  • P or Esc pauses, M toggles sound.
  • Space or Enter plays again after a fall.

Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. The game makes no network requests and has no ads or tracking.
