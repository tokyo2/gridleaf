Sky Hopper

Best: 0

How to play

  • You ride the rim of a spinning island.
  • Tap (or press Space) to leap straight out from where you stand.
  • Wait until you're facing the next island, then leap and land on it.
  • Collect golden seeds for +10. Cracked islands crumble after one full turn, so leap before they go.
  • Fall below the screen and the run is over. P pauses.
