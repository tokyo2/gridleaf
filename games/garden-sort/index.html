Garden Sort

Sort seedlings into pots, but you may only plant on a matching color or shape. 100 cozy puzzles.

Play now

About the game

Garden Sort is a cozy sorting puzzle with a twist. Each seedling has a color and a shape. Lift the top seedling from a pot and plant it on an empty pot, or on a seedling that shares its color or its shape. Fill each pot with four of one kind to finish the garden.

  • 100 levels, from three kinds to eight; the color-or-shape twist starts on level 3
  • Every level is built backwards from a solved garden, so it's always solvable
  • Free undo and a smart hint button
  • Beat par for three stars
  • A short in-game tutorial on the first level
  • Touch, mouse and keyboard; phones and desktops
  • Soft sounds you can mute

How to play

  • Tap a pot to lift its top seedling, then tap another pot to plant it.
  • Keyboard: number keys pick pots (0 is pot 10), U undo, H hint, R restart, M sound, P menu.

Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. The game makes no network requests and has no ads or tracking.
