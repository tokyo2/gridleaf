Garden Sort

Move seedlings between pots until every pot holds one kind.

How to play

  • Tap a pot to pick up its top seedling, then tap another pot to plant it.
  • You can plant on an empty pot, or on a seedling that shares its colour or its shape.
  • A pot holds four. Sort until each full pot has one kind (same colour and shape).
  • Stuck? Undo is free (it takes the move back), or tap Hint. Finish within par for three stars.
  • Keyboard: number keys pick pots (0 is pot 10), U undo, H hint, R restart, M sound, P menu.
