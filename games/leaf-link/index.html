About the game

Leaf Link is a calm tile-turning puzzle. Each board is a little garden: a clay pot sits at the root, and vine tiles are scattered in every direction. Tap a tile to turn it and link the vines back to the root. When every tile is connected with no loose ends, the buds burst into flowers.

  • 120 generated levels that grow from 3×3 to 8×8, with rocky gaps in later gardens
  • Every level is verified solvable
  • Beat par for three stars; press and hold (or right-click) turns a tile back in one move
  • A short in-game tutorial on the first level
  • Level select and saved progress
  • Works with touch, mouse and keyboard, on phones and desktops
  • Gentle sounds you can mute

How to play

  • Tap or click a tile to turn it clockwise.
  • Press and hold (touch) or right-click to turn it back.
  • Keyboard: arrows to move, Space to turn, X to turn back, R restart, M sound, P menu.

Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. The game makes no network requests and has no ads or tracking.
