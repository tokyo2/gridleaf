Leaf Link

Turn the tiles so every vine grows from the root to a flower.

How to play

  • The clay pot is the root. Vines grow from it.
  • Tap a tile to turn it clockwise. Right-click (or press X) turns it the other way.
  • Connect every tile to the root with no loose vine ends. Buds bloom when they're linked.
  • Finish within par moves for three stars.
  • Keyboard: arrows to move, Space to turn, X to turn back, R to restart, P to pause.
