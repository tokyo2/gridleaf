Questions, answered

How do I get my files?

Straight after checkout you receive an email with a download link. You can download the files again at any time from that email.

Which formats are included?

Each product page lists its formats beside the buy button, for example PDF in Letter and A4 sizes, or SVG and PNG for design assets.

Do I get updates?

Yes. When a product is improved, the new version is added to your download at no extra cost.

What if a product isn't right for me?

If a download does not work as its page describes, write to us and we will fix it or refund you.

Can I use the products for my business?

Each product page has a short licence section. Design assets and business templates can be used commercially; printables are for personal use.
