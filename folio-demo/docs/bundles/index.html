Bundles

Sell several products together; Folio lists what is included and works out the saving.

A bundle is a normal product file with two extra fields:

---
title: Creator Starter Bundle
description: The planner, the icon set and the proposal kit together.
price: 39
image: ../../assets/products/starter-bundle.png
imageAlt: Covers of the three products in the bundle
buyUrl: https://YOURNAME.gumroad.com/l/bundle
layout: bundle
includes: [weekly-planner, icon-set, proposal-kit]
---

includes lists product file names without .md. The bundle page then shows:

  • a “What’s included” list with each product’s image, description, price and link,
  • the combined value and the saving, for example “Worth $55 separately, you save $16 (29%)”,
  • a “Bundle” badge on the product card.

If an id in includes does not match a product file, the build stops and tells you which one. The saving line is hidden when the bundle costs the same as or more than its parts.

Create the bundle itself on your checkout platform as one product (most platforms let you attach several files), then paste its link as buyUrl.
