Before launch

A complete checklist of everything that still shows demo content, so nothing sample goes live.

Work through this list once before you publish. Everything here is either in src/folio.config.ts or a file you replace.

In folio.config.ts

  • site: name, tagline, description, url (your real address), author, lang and locale.
  • site.demoMode: false. This hides the “Sample” testimonials and any form that is not connected.
  • nav, socials and footer (copyright line, links). Social links can be any URL.
  • home.hero: eyebrow, title, text, buttons, image (a file in src/assets/) and imageAlt (what the image shows).
  • home.titles, home.about and home.sections (remove what you don’t need).
  • shop: currency, title, intro, relatedTitle, bundleTitle.
  • contact: email, intro, notes and, if you want a form, form.action.
  • newsletter.action and emailField from your provider, or newsletter.enabled: false.
  • pricing: enabled, title, intro, faqTitle.
  • docs.enabled: false unless you replaced these pages with your own help pages.
  • footer.showCredit: true adds a small “Built with Folio” link; leave false to hide it.

Content files

  • src/content/products/: replace the demo products and their checkout links (buyUrl).
  • src/assets/products/ and src/assets/blog/: replace the demo images. Delete src/assets/hero.png if you set a different hero image.
  • src/content/blog/: delete the demo posts, including the formatting guide.
  • src/pages/about.md: your own story.
  • src/content/testimonials.yaml: real quotes you have permission to publish, or [] to hide the section.
  • src/content/faq.yaml: your own answers. The demo FAQ mentions refunds and updates: write your actual policies or delete those entries.
  • src/content/tiers.yaml: your plans and checkout links, or [] to hide the pricing blocks.
  • src/content/docs/: your own help pages (or docs.enabled: false).

Files in public/

  • public/og-default.png: the image shown when a page without its own image is shared (1200 × 630).
  • public/favicon.svg: the browser tab icon.
  • public/apple-touch-icon.png: the icon for phone home screens (180 × 180).

Optional text in pages

  • src/pages/404.astro: the “page not found” wording.
  • src/pages/contact.astro, src/pages/blog/[...page].astro: small labels such as “Contact” and “Blog”.

Last checks

  • npm run check:contrast if you changed colours.
  • npm run build, then npm run preview and click through every page.
  • Share a product link in a messaging app to check the sharing image and title.
