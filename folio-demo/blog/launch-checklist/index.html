A simple launch checklist for digital products

Twelve small checks that catch most launch-day problems, from broken download links to missing alt text.

By Folio DemoSeptember 30, 20261 min read

Launch day goes better when the boring checks are done the day before. Here is the list we use.

The product

  • Download the file yourself from a real test purchase.
  • Open it on a phone and on a computer.
  • Check the file name: clear, no “final-v3”.

The page

  • The title says what it is.
  • The price and the formats are visible without scrolling.
  • Every image has alt text that describes it.
  • The buy button goes to the right checkout.

The site

  • The product appears in the shop grid and in its category.
  • The page has a sharing image (paste the link into a messaging app to check).
  • The site builds without warnings: npm run build.

After launch

  • Tell your newsletter and link to the product page, not the checkout.
  • Write down what you learned while it is fresh.

Nothing on this list is difficult. It is just easy to forget when you are excited to share something new.
