How to get the most out of it

  • On 14-count Aida the tree is about 2.6 × 3.3 in (6.5 × 8.3 cm). A 5 in hoop fits it on 14 count, a 4 in hoop on 18 count.
  • Start in the center: fold the fabric in quarters and match the arrows on the edges of the chart.
  • Use 2 strands of floss for each stitch (1 or 2 on 18 count) and keep your thread about 18 in (45 cm) long.
  • Print the black-and-white symbol chart and tick off squares as you stitch.
  • Finish it as a felt-backed ornament, a little pillow ornament or in a mini hoop. The PDF explains each one.

What you need

  • 14-count Aida in white or cream (16 or 18 count for a smaller ornament). The PDF gives the piece to cut for each count.
  • DMC six-strand embroidery floss in the 9 colors listed here
  • A blunt tapestry needle: size 24 for 14 count, size 26 for 16 and 18 count
  • An embroidery hoop (optional) and small, sharp scissors

Free printable Christmas cross-stitch pattern FAQ

Is the pattern really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and stitch it as often as you like, for yourself or as handmade gifts. Please link to this page rather than sharing the file.

Is it good for beginners?

Yes. It uses whole cross stitches only (772 of them), with no backstitch, and the how-to page explains how to read the chart, start in the center and finish neatly.

What size is the finished ornament?

About 2.6 × 3.3 in (6.5 × 8.3 cm) on 14-count Aida and 2.0 × 2.6 in (5.1 × 6.5 cm) on 18 count. The PDF also lists the size and fabric to cut for 16 count.

Which threads do I need?

Nine DMC colors: 699, 701, 890, 725, 783, 321, 3843, 434, 801. One skein of each is plenty, and any brand works with a conversion chart.

Which paper size should I print?

US Letter for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Print at 100% / Actual size.
