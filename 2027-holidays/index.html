2027 holidays FAQ

How many federal holidays are there in 2027?

There are 11 US federal holidays in 2027.

Which 2027 holidays fall on a weekend?

Juneteenth (Saturday, June 19) is observed on Friday, June 18; Independence Day (Sunday, July 4) is observed on Monday, July 5; and Christmas Day (Saturday, December 25) is observed on Friday, December 24. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

How many long weekends are there in 2027?

9 federal holidays are observed on a Monday or Friday in 2027, giving a three-day weekend: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day / Indigenous Peoples' Day, Christmas Day.

When is Easter 2027?

Easter Sunday is March 28, 2027.

When is Thanksgiving 2027?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 25, 2027.
