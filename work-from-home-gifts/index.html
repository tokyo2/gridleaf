Gift guide FAQ

What's a good gift for someone who works from home?

Something that fixes a daily annoyance: a laptop stand or footrest for comfort, a light or headset for video calls, or a cable kit and desk organizer for clutter. Ask a casual question about their setup first, so the gift fits.

What helps most on video calls?

Light in front of the face rather than behind it, and a headset or microphone closer to the mouth than a laptop's built-in one. A webcam at eye level helps too, which a laptop stand makes easy.

How should a home desk be set up?

Common ergonomic advice is to put the top of the screen at or slightly below eye level, keep the keyboard close enough that the elbows stay near the body and rest the feet flat on the floor or a footrest.
