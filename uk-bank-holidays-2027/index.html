UK bank holidays FAQ

How many bank holidays are there in 2027 in England?

England and Wales have 8 bank holidays in 2027.

When is Easter 2027 in the UK?

Good Friday is 26 March 2027 and Easter Monday is 29 March 2027.

When are the Christmas bank holidays in 2027?

Christmas Day falls on Saturday 25 December and Boxing Day on Sunday 26 December 2027, so the substitute bank holidays are Monday 27 and Tuesday 28 December.

Are these dates official?

They follow the standard rules used by the UK government. Occasionally an extra one-off bank holiday is announced, so check GOV.UK closer to the date.
