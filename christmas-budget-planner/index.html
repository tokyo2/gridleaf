Costs people forget

These are the lines that turn a careful budget into a January surprise. Give each one a number, even a small one.

  • Wrapping paper, tape and tags
  • Postage for cards and parcels
  • Batteries for new toys
  • Teacher, coach and coworker gifts
  • Secret Santa and gift exchanges
  • Hosting extras: drinks, ice and disposables
  • Travel: fuel, parking, pet sitting
  • Holiday outfits and photos

Ways to spend less without missing out

  • Draw names for a Secret Santa among the adults instead of buying for everyone.
  • Set one per-person limit and tell the family, so nobody feels they have to match.
  • For kids, try the want, need, wear and read rule: four gifts, each with a purpose.
  • Give homemade treats, a framed photo or a handmade ornament: try our free Christmas cross-stitch pattern.
  • Look for ideas under a set price, like our stocking stuffers under $25.
  • Buy next year's wrapping paper and cards in the January sales.

How to make a Christmas budget in 4 steps

  1. Find your number. Add what you have already saved to what you can set aside from each paycheck until mid-December. That total is your budget, whatever the ads say.
  2. List everyone you buy for on the free Christmas gift list, then choose an amount per person that fits.
  3. Split the rest between food, decorations, cards and postage, travel and giving, and keep 5–10% for Other.
  4. Track as you go. Write each purchase on the spending log the same day and check it each weekend.

Free printable Christmas budget planner FAQ

How much should I spend on Christmas?

There's no single right number. A safe budget is one you can pay for by mid-December without carrying a credit card balance into January: what you've already saved plus what you can set aside from each paycheck until then. Put your numbers in the calculator above to see what that means per week.

How much should I spend on Christmas gifts per child?

Pick an amount that fits your total budget rather than what other families spend, and keep it the same for each child so it feels fair. Many families also limit the number of gifts with the want, need, wear and read rule: something they want, something they need, something to wear and something to read.

What should a Christmas budget include?

Gifts for family and friends, small gifts for teachers, hosts and coworkers, stocking stuffers, holiday meals and treats, decorations, cards and postage, wrapping, travel, outfits and events, giving, and a little for other. The printable worksheet has a line for each one.

When should I start saving for Christmas?

As early as you can, because the earlier you start, the smaller each payment. Starting in the first week of January gives you about 51 weeks, so a $600 Christmas costs under $12 a week. Starting in November leaves only about six or seven weeks.
