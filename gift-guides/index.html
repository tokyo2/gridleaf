Gift guide FAQ

When should I start holiday shopping?

Christmas Day 2026 is Friday, December 25, and Black Friday is November 27. Make your list and budget first, then buy anything that has to be shipped early. Delivery cut-off dates vary by store and carrier, so check them for gifts that must arrive by a certain day.

How do you choose the ideas in these guides?

We pick kinds of gifts that suit each person and explain what to look for in each. We haven't tested specific products. The guides link to Amazon search results, and some links earn us a commission: see our disclosures.

How can I keep track of who I'm buying for?

Print the free Christmas gift list: it has columns for the gift idea, budget, amount spent, and bought and wrapped checkboxes.
