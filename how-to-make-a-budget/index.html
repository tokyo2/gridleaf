How to Make a Budget in 6 Steps

A budget is a plan for your money before the month starts. This method takes an afternoon to set up and about ten minutes a week to keep going.

  1. Know your real take-home pay. Use what actually lands in your account each month. If it varies, use your lowest month from the past six.
  2. List your fixed bills. Rent, utilities, insurance, phone, subscriptions, minimum debt payments: everything that's the same every month.
  3. Track one month of spending. Write down or import every transaction. Group them into 8–12 categories; more than that gets hard to keep up.
  4. Give every dollar a job. Set a monthly target for each category so income minus all targets is zero. Savings is a category too.
  5. Plan for irregular costs. Car repairs, gifts and annual fees aren't surprises. Divide them by 12 and save monthly in sinking funds.
  6. Review monthly. At month end, compare budget with actual, move money between categories, and adjust next month's targets.
