Tips

  • Introduce one or two words a day and use them together in a short sentence.
  • Point out the word in picture books after the worksheet: finding it “in the wild” makes it stick.
  • Review with quick flashcard games such as snap, memory or “beat the timer”.

FAQ

What are sight words?

Common words like “the” and “and” that children learn to recognize instantly, because many can't be sounded out.

Is there an answer key?

Yes, the counts for the “find it” rows are printed at the bottom of the page.

Where can I get all the words?

The full pack covers all 133 Dolch pre-primer, primer and first grade words, with flashcards.

Which paper size should I print?
