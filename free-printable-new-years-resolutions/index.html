How to get the most out of it

  • Write resolutions as actions (“walk 3 times a week”), not wishes (“get fit”).
  • The Why box matters most. Reread it when motivation dips in February.
  • Break each plan into steps small enough to start this week.
  • Shade a tracker circle at the end of each month you kept it up.
