How to Use a Digital Planner in GoodNotes or Notability

A digital planner is a PDF with links: tap a date and you jump to that day. Here's how to set one up in five minutes.

  1. Download the PDF. On your iPad or tablet, open the download link and save the PDF to Files (iPad) or Downloads (Android).
  2. Import it into your app. In GoodNotes: tap + then Import and pick the PDF. In Notability: tap Import, then choose the file. It opens as one notebook.
  3. Switch to read-only to use the links. Hyperlinks work when you're not in writing mode. In GoodNotes, tap the pen icon to toggle; in Notability, tap the hand or read mode.
  4. Bookmark the pages you use most. Add bookmarks for this month and this week so they're one tap away, alongside the planner's own side tabs.
  5. Write, highlight and add stickers. Use the pen and highlighter tools like paper. Many people keep one colour per area of life.

Which apps work?

Any PDF app that supports hyperlinks: GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf, Xodo, Flexcil, PDF Expert, Samsung Notes and Adobe Acrobat. On Android, Samsung Notes and Xodo are popular free options.

Digital or printable?

Digital planners are lighter, searchable and endlessly erasable; printables are better if you like real paper. You can try both for free: the free printable planner pages also import into GoodNotes.
