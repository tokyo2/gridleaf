Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

How many players can play?

This free set has 8 different cards, so up to 8 players or teams. Each card has a different mix of pictures, and no two cards share a row, column or diagonal.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. The squares are pictures with simple labels, so younger kids can play with a little help.
