2027 calendar FAQ

What day of the week does 2027 start on?

January 1, 2027 is a Friday, and December 31, 2027 is a Friday.

Is 2027 a leap year?

No. 2027 has 365 days and February has 28 days. The next leap year is 2028.

How many weeks are in 2027?

2027 has 52 full weeks plus 1 day. In ISO week numbering (weeks start on Monday) it has 52 weeks.

Can I print this calendar for free?

Yes. The PDFs are free for personal use — print as many copies as you like for your home, classroom fridge or desk.

Which paper size should I pick?

US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for the US and Canada, A4 for most other countries. Both print landscape.
