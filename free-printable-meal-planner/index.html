How to get the most out of it

  • Check what's already in the fridge and pantry before you plan.
  • Plan two or three “cook once, eat twice” dinners and use leftovers for lunch.
  • Group the grocery list by store section so you shop faster.
  • Leave one night free for takeout or leftovers. Plans that allow for real life are the ones that stick.

Free printable meal planner FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Download the PDF and print as many copies as you like for personal, home or classroom use. No sign-up, no email.

What's on the page?

A 7-day grid with breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, plus a three-column grocery list with checkboxes.

Does the week start on Sunday or Monday?

The US Letter version starts the week on Sunday and the A4 version starts on Monday.

Can I use it in GoodNotes or Notability?

Yes. Import the PDF into GoodNotes, Notability or any PDF app, then duplicate the page whenever you need a fresh one.
