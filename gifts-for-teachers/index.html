Budget tips

  • Set a budget for each person and write it on the free printable Christmas gift list before you shop.

Gift guide FAQ

How much should parents spend on a teacher gift?

There's no set amount, and a heartfelt card often means as much as anything bought. Check your school's gift policy first, and if families want to give more, a group gift from the class lets everyone chip in a little.

Are gift cards a good teacher gift?

They let the teacher choose what they need. A card for a local coffee shop, bookstore or supply store works well, especially with a short note from your child.

What should I avoid giving a teacher?

Strong scents and homemade food can be tricky because of allergies and preferences. Choose something unscented, or ask the class parent rep what the teacher likes.

How did you choose these ideas?

We picked kinds of gifts that suit teachers and wrote down what to look for in each one. We haven't tested specific brands or products. The links open Amazon search results, so you can compare options, prices and reviews yourself.
