Does it work on Notion's free plan?

Yes. It's built with standard Notion pages, databases, views and formulas, so a free Notion account is all you need. You don't need a paid plan or any add-ons.

Can I change the habits?

Yes. Rename the six habit columns (Move 30 min, Read 20 min, Drink water, Meditate, Journal and Bed by 11) to your own and the daily score keeps working. The dashboard shows how to add a seventh habit. Rename any other property too; just keep the goal status Done as it is, because the Days left formula checks for it.

How is the daily score worked out?

It's the share of your six habits you ticked that day, from 0 to 100: four out of six gives 67.
