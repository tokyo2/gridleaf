Tie-breaker

Counting every repeat, how many gifts are given in total in the carol "The Twelve Days of Christmas"? Closest answer wins.

Show answer

364. Gift number k is given on every day from day k to day 12, so the total is 1×12 + 2×11 + … + 12×1 = 364.

Free printable Christmas trivia FAQ

How do you play Christmas trivia?

Split into teams, read each question and its three options aloud, and let teams circle A, B or C on the answer sheet. Score one point per correct answer; the tie-breaker settles a draw.

Are the answers included?

Yes. Tap Show answer under each question on this page, and the PDF has a one-page answer key for the host.

Is it good for kids?

Round 3 is an easy Kids' Round for about ages 5 to 9. Older kids can usually manage rounds 1 and 2 with a little help from their team.

When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 is Friday, December 25, 2026, so Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24.

Can I use it at school, church or work?

Yes. Print as many copies as you need for your own party, classroom or group.
